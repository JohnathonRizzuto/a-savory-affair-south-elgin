Memberships

BECOME A MEMBER

Two ways to join. Both come with a vote on who fills our board seats, and both put you behind every sport at Sandwich High School.

Gold Booster
$40
  • Direct your fee to a sport. Fulfil one qualifying volunteer slot — a JV or Varsity football game, or one shift in the Sandwich Fair booth — and your $40 goes to the sport you choose.
  • 2 Booster Event Passes. Free access to two home SHS athletic events of your choosing. Excludes post-season play.
  • Earn more passes. Volunteer at more than one booster event to earn additional passes — one pass per volunteer slot.
  • Voting privileges on filling Sandwich Boosters board positions.
  • Express concessions line. Access to the Gold Member Express Concessions Line at home football games when available. Member card required.
Basic Booster
FREE
  • No fee. Basic Membership begins the first time you volunteer.
  • Earn Booster Event Passes. Volunteer at more than one booster event to earn passes — one pass gets you free access to one home SHS athletic event. Excludes post-season play.
  • Voting privileges on filling Sandwich Boosters board positions.

Volunteering isn't required, but it's encouraged. If you choose not to volunteer you can't direct your membership fee to a sport — you still get every other benefit.
