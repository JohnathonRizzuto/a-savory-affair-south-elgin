FOR SHS COACHES

If your program needs something, this is where you start. The Boosters exist to buy the things the school budget doesn't cover.

Every piece of equipment we've bought started the same way — a coach told us what their program needed. The $40,000 gym projector, the wrestling mats, the pitching machine, the volleyball netting: all of it came from a request.

01

TELL US WHAT YOU NEED

Submit a request with the item, roughly what it costs, and which program it's for.

02

WE REVIEW IT

Requests go to the board and get weighed against what's already committed for the season.

03

WE FUND WHAT WE CAN

Approved requests get purchased. That's how everything on the About Us page happened.
